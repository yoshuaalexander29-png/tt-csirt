Cyber security in Trinidad and Tobago

The Trinidad & Tobago Cyber Security Incident Response Team (TT-CSIRT) helps citizens, public-sector organisations and private-sector organisations respond to cyber security incidents and reduce cyber risks.

Need support?

If you are experiencing an active cyber attack, ransomware infection, or suspected data compromise, contact the national response team immediately.

Report a cyber incident
What happens when you report an incident to TT-CSIRT?

Our incident handling team will triage your report within 1 hour. We provide technical mitigation advice, containment strategies, and coordination with law enforcement or international CSIRTs where appropriate.

All reports are handled under strict confidentiality protocols in accordance with the National Cyber Security Framework.

What TT-CSIRT does

TT-CSIRT coordinates national cyber incident response and publishes trusted guidance to help people and organisations manage cyber security risks.

TT-CSIRT has a national remit across the public and private sectors and was established in November 2015. Its work is guided by the National Cyber Security Strategy (2012).

Working with partners

TT-CSIRT coordinates with national, regional and international partners to share expertise, information and support.
